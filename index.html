DATA SHIFT · An Epilogue

HIS MEMORY

He could not keep his own data.
So he left it here, for you.

Two memories. Watch them in order.

I The Frame

He knew the order. Corners first, then the lines.
The frame is everyone's.

Memory one, the frame: he paints the three corner squares and the timing lines that every code carries. The frame is everyone's.

II The Words

The frame was already his.
The words are what he could not keep.

Memory two, the words: he fills in the data area. The finished code reads: HE REMEMBERS YOU.

Take it back to him.

Start again. He is waiting.
